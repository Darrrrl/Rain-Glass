# Bundled sound sources

All recordings below were published by their authors as public domain or CC0 on Wikimedia Commons. The bundled AAC files are shortened, compressed derivatives of the originals. Ambient sources were split into three overlapping variants per layer; the room recording was low-pass filtered to make it a quiet indoor bed. Thunder files were trimmed to begin at the audible thunder onset or rumble. No source recordings are loaded from the network at runtime.
